v2

Fixed a hard crash on the newest experimental UE4SS builds. Pressing Equip/Remove in the
weapon menu killed the game instantly. Older UE4SS builds were unaffected, so this only hit
people who had updated.

The cause was the mod reading the skin reference off the weapon at runtime. The new UE4SS
changed how that kind of reference is stored internally. v2 no longer reads it at all -
skin paths now come from a list built from the game's own weapon data. Tested working on
both the UE4SS build linked on the mod page and the latest experimental one.

Also fixed two Verso weapons, Velokan and Mitigated Perfection, that applied no skin at
all. Their weapon data sits in a different folder from every other weapon and was being
skipped when the skin list was built.

Also removed the extra logging that was added while tracking the crash down.
